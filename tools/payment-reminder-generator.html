How to remind a client about payment without ruining the relationship

  
Keep the first reminder short and assume good faith — most late payments are forgetfulness, not refusal. Reference the invoice number and amount precisely, restate the due date, and make replying easy. Escalate tone only with time: a firm status request at one week, a final notice at two — and say plainly what happens next (paused work) without threats.

  
When should I send a payment reminder in India?

  
On the due date itself, then day 7, then day 14. Under MSME rules many buyers owe interest on delayed payments to registered suppliers — worth mentioning only at the final stage, and only if it applies to you.
